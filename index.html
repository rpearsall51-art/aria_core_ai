SeedSphere Autonomous Reforestation System

The SeedSphere is a reusable, drone-deployable spherical robot engineered to plant native California species across wildfire burn scars. Each SeedSphere aerates the soil, distributes native seeds, and signals for retrieval—ensuring no hardware is ever left behind.

  • Drone deployable & drone retrievable
  • 0.5–1 acre treated per mission
  • Native California seeds: Ponderosa Pine, Incense Cedar, Jeffrey Pine
  • Completely reusable | Zero forest litter

Aria Enterprise Drone

A heavy-lift autonomous platform designed for rugged terrain, the Aria Enterprise Drone deploys and retrieves 10–12 SeedSpheres per sortie. Using AI-assisted terrain mapping and mission planning, it can restore 5–12 acres per flight.

  • Dual SeedSphere payload tubes
  • Precision drop-gate + retrieval funnel system
  • Autonomous mapping & route planning
  • Up to 280,000 native seeds planted per day with continuous cycling

Our Mission

Based in Railroad Flat, Calaveras County, AriaCore is dedicated to restoring California forests through robotics and AI-driven automation. We support CAL FIRE, Sierra Nevada Conservancy, timber companies, and private landowners in accelerating post-fire ecosystem recovery.
