SeedSphere Autonomous Reforestation System

The SeedSphere is a reusable, drone-deployable ground unit engineered for planting native California species across wildfire burn scars and degraded terrain. Each sphere aerates soil, distributes native seeds, and then signals for retrieval—ensuring no hardware is left behind.

  • Drone deployable + retrievable
  • 0.5–1 acre treated per SeedSphere mission
  • Fully reusable and environmentally safe
  • Supports Ponderosa Pine, Incense Cedar, Jeffrey Pine & more

Aria Enterprise Drone

The Aria Enterprise Drone is a heavy-lift autonomous aircraft designed for rugged post-fire terrain. With dual SeedSphere payload tubes (10–12 spheres per sortie), the system can plant between 5–12 acres per mission and operate continuously for large-scale recovery.

  • Precision mapping and terrain-aware deployment
  • Automated SeedSphere drop and retrieval funnels
  • Operates in hazardous areas unsafe for manual crews
  • Up to 280,000 native seeds planted per day with continuous cycling

Our Mission

Based in Railroad Flat, Calaveras County, AriaCore is dedicated to restoring California’s forests through advanced robotics and autonomous aerial systems. Our technologies support CAL FIRE, Sierra Nevada Conservancy, private landowners, and local communities in accelerating ecosystem recovery and strengthening climate resilience.
